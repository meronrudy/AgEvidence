# Build an Assurance Platform

## The Business

Turn assurance work into a software-enabled workflow while keeping reviewer judgment recoverable.

## Who Pays

Assurance providers, auditors, processors, retailers, and programs that need repeatable evidence review.

## The Incumbent Workflow Being Unbundled

Assurance work often happens across spreadsheets, email, PDFs, and bespoke portals. This recipe turns review, gaps, determinations, and artifacts into the product.

## What Information Enters Your Product

Source documents, audit evidence, observations, reviewer notes, requirement mappings, exceptions, determinations, and signed-off artifacts.

## AgEvidence Primitives

Use `SourceRecord` for custody, `EvidenceRecord` for normalized evidence, `Gap` for exceptions, `Review` for human decisions, `Determination` for bounded conclusions, and `Artifact` for portable outputs.

## Rails Screens To Keep

Keep projects, source records, evidence, gaps, reviews, determinations, artifacts, verification, reliance, organization access, and developer logs.

## Rails Screens To Delete

Delete only screens that distract from reviewer throughput after the assurance workflow is focused.

## What You Rename

Rename projects to assurance engagements, gaps to exceptions, determinations to assurance conclusions, and artifacts to assurance packages.

## What You Build Yourself

Build reviewer assignment, sampling, audit planning, client portals, evidence request workflows, and assurance-firm reporting.

## First API Integration

Accept source records from a client system or document collection workflow.

## First Evidence Artifact

Issue an assurance package with explicit limitations and reviewer decisions.

## First External Verifier

Let a client or buyer verify that the package has not changed after issuance.

## Proprietary Moat

Reviewer network, assurance methodology, client workflow, sector-specific templates, and operational throughput.

## Keep Interoperable

Source records, review decisions, determinations, artifacts, verification results, and reliance events.
