# Build a Biological Input Evidence Startup

## The Business

Make input efficacy evidence portable across manufacturers, field trials, distributors, growers, and procurement teams.

## Who Pays

Biological input companies, crop input distributors, retailers, and buyers who need defensible efficacy claims.

## The Incumbent Workflow Being Unbundled

Input evidence is often trapped in manufacturer PDFs, trial portals, and sales collateral. This recipe turns trial and field evidence into reusable artifacts.

## What Information Enters Your Product

Product lots, field applications, trial protocols, crop observations, soil and tissue tests, yield outcomes, weather records, model outputs, and reviewer conclusions.

## AgEvidence Primitives

Use `SourceRecord` for trial custody, `InterventionEvent` for applications, `Observation` for crop and soil measurements, `OperationalEvent` for management context, and `Determination` for bounded efficacy statements.

## Rails Screens To Keep

Keep source records, evidence, gaps, assessment, review, determinations, artifacts, verification, ProgramProfiles, schemas, OpenAPI, and webhooks.

## Rails Screens To Delete

Delete livestock-only terminology after the biological input ProgramProfile is in place.

## What You Rename

Rename intervention events to application events, projects to trial evidence cases, gaps to claim-readiness checks, and artifacts to efficacy evidence packages.

## What You Build Yourself

Build trial importers, agronomy workflow screens, field map context, distributor sharing, and crop-specific analytics.

## First API Integration

Accept trial observations and application events from a trial management system.

## First Evidence Artifact

Issue a bounded efficacy artifact for one product, crop, geography, and season.

## First External Verifier

Allow a distributor or procurement team to reconstruct the artifact boundary and evidence commitments.

## Proprietary Moat

Trial network access, agronomic interpretation, crop-specific analytics, manufacturer distribution, and procurement relationships.

## Keep Interoperable

Source records, observations, intervention events, determinations, artifacts, and verification results.
