# Observations

An observation records a measurement or physical assertion about a subject.

Research examples use observations for:

- CH4, CO2, and H2 production and yield
- dry matter intake
- body weight and average daily gain
- carcass and milk measurements
- feed composition fields such as NDF, CP, EE, OM, and DMI
- reported SEM, reported SD, and derived SD values

Each observation points back to source records. Case-study examples also record
limitations when public row-level data are not available.
