# Kebreab Research Guide

This guide is built around actual research objects and public datasets from
Dr. Ermias Kebreab and collaborators.

Progression:

1. [Roque et al. 2021 Asparagopsis](roque-2021.md): full executable public
   workbook tutorial.
2. [Ramirez-Agudelo and Kebreab 2024 methane database](methane-database-2024.md):
   full executable Zenodo workbook tutorial.
3. [GreenFeed provenance](../workflows/greenfeed-provenance.md): instrument
   provenance mapping from published methods.
4. [Akter et al. 2025 grape pomace](grape-pomace-2025.md): Latin-square schema
   and provenance case study.
5. [Meo-Filho et al. 2024 grazing beef](grazing-beef-2024.md): field-study
   reconstruction from published results.
6. [Niu et al. intercontinental model](intercontinental-model.md): multi-source
   model-provenance case study.
7. [RationSmart](rationsmart.md): feed database to methane-accounting
   architecture.

The first two workbooks are downloaded from public repositories. Later chapters
do not pretend that private row-level lab data are public.
