# Grazing Beef 2024

Meo-Filho, Ramirez-Agudelo, and Kebreab studied a pelleted
Asparagopsis-derived feed additive under grazing beef conditions.

- Article DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2410863121
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39621924/

Public sources describe 24 Wagyu x Angus steers, control and Brominata groups,
14-day weigh-ins, GreenFeed CH4/CO2/H2 measurement, SAS 9.4 analysis, and
phase-dependent bromoform intake.

This chapter is a field-evidence workflow:

```text
controlled feeding trial
  -> grazing field trial
  -> repeated GreenFeed visits
  -> phase-dependent intervention
  -> field-valid statement with limitations
```

The branch treats this as a published-result reconstruction because the article
states study data are in the main text rather than exposing a separate row-level
workbook.
